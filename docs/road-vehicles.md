# Electric road vehicles

The Electric Golf Cart seats four and uses one existing 48 V 20 kWh Traction Battery. Its maximum speed is 36 km/h. It climbs a one-block step, including the energy needed to lift the vehicle. A two-block wall stops it. Sustained sharp turns above approximately 27 km/h can destroy it in an explosion and start a fire. Slow down before corners.

The Arc Runner Coupe seats two. Install six traction batteries for its 120 kWh bank. Its maximum speed is 162 km/h, with acceleration limited by motor power and remaining battery energy. Both vehicles have nine storage slots.

Place a vehicle on clear ground. Sneak-right-click it while parked and empty to open its battery bays and storage. Insert charged traction batteries; newly crafted packs are empty. Right-click to board, use the normal movement keys to drive, and sneak to dismount. The first rider drives. Battery energy is saved in each installed item and survives vehicle saves.

## Charging installed batteries

Connect the blue left or bottom power terminal of a **240 V 100 A Vehicle Charger** to a protected 240 V supply. It accepts fixed electrical cable rather than a lower-current household appliance cord. At nominal voltage its maximum demand is 100 A / 24 kW.

Use a **100 A Vehicle Charging Lead** on the charger, then right-click a parked, unoccupied vehicle within six blocks with that lead. Sneak-right-click the vehicle with the lead to disconnect. A plugged-in vehicle cannot drive or have its traction batteries removed. Charging shares measured input energy across the installed packs, respects their charge ratings and remaining capacity, and stores 94% of the incoming energy. Full batteries stop the load.

A broken or unloaded charger releases the vehicle. The connection does not force chunks to load and must be reconnected after a world reload. Supply interruptions stop charging. The charger readouts and multimeter report input voltage/current and power.

All new item, entity and node identifiers are appended; existing industrial equipment and the Four Cell Plug-in UPS retain their IDs.
